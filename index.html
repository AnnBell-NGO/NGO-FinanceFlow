A fictional NGO initiative supporting resilient communities through practical skills, inclusive local partnerships and slightly over-ambitious impact statements.

Communities thrive in a world where nobody is left behind by the global transition to sustainable sandwich-making.

Donor partners

Northstar Foundation

Supports youth skills and community-led innovation. Its strategic priority is “turning potential into practical possibility”, preferably with measurable indicators.

Global Neighbourhood Fund

Funds inclusive local partnerships and resilient communities. Particularly interested in collaboration, sustainability and evidence that people actually attend the workshops.

European Green Horizons Facility

Supports circular economy, low-waste pilots and green skills. Requires clear project outputs and transparent expenditure reporting.

Project at a glance

Purpose
Help local organisations and residents build practical skills, access opportunities and test sustainable community solutions.

Who benefits?
Young people, local NGOs, community groups, small enterprises and the occasional overworked project coordinator.

How we work
Training, partnerships, small pilots, peer learning and continuous feedback.
